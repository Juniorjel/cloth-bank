## 2. 💼 LINKEDIN POST

### Recommended Media:
* Document Carousel (PDF of `poster.html` or `PROJECT_DRAFT.md`) OR Video: `cloth_bank_promo_video.webp`

### Copy / Caption:
```text
How can tech-driven logistics solve the friction in disaster relief donation? 🏔️📦

During climate emergencies like the recent Rasuwa floods in Nepal, hundreds of families lose basic necessities within minutes. While urban citizens have closets full of wearable, warm clothing ready to donate, participation drops due to one major bottleneck:

👉 The Working Professional's Dilemma:
Individuals working 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM jobs cannot visit distant physical collection camps during regular hours. As a result, usable clothes sit idle while disaster victims freeze.

To bridge this gap, we built Cloth Bank (कपडा बैंक) — a digital humanitarian logistics and clothing bank management platform. 🧺

Key Innovations:
🔹 On-Demand Doorstep Pickup: Donors drop a pin via Google Maps and schedule an automated pickup from their home or office.
🔹 Dedicated Field Agent App: Mobile dispatch queue with GPS navigation and route optimization for relief drivers.
🔹 End-to-End Campaign Tracking: Donors, NGOs, and volunteers can monitor exact metrics in real time (e.g., "1,560 / 2,000 clothing sets collected for Rasuwa Flood Relief").
🔹 Categorization & Verification: Items are sorted into Kids, Women, Men, and Winter wear before dispatch, eliminating chaotic donation piles.

Technology should make doing good frictionless. By digitizing the donation supply chain, Cloth Bank ensures no surplus clothing is wasted, and relief reaches flood-affected families faster.

💻 Built with Laravel + Vue.js for scalable NGO logistics.

#SocialImpact #DisasterRelief #TechForGood #SupplyChainLogistics #NepalTech #CircularEconomy #ESG #HumanitarianAid #WebDevelopment #ClothBank
```
